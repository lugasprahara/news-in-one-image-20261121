## Method note
Every kept story was pinned to Saturday by the source's own dateline, timestamp or "on Saturday" wording — never by an aggregator's day-page label. Aggregator day pages (OneTurn, Daily Short, AfricaFrontPage, C54, BBC day pages, school-assembly roundups) were used only as lead generators; each lead was then checked against the underlying article. Where a figure was still moving (bitcoin), the Saturday-GMT wire updates are quoted with their timestamps; where outlets differed (Peru quake depth; Australia rally turnout), the range or the lower figure is used and noted.
